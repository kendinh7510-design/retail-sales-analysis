Retail Sales & Retention Analysis

Overview:
Analysis of retail transaction data (30,000+ order line items, 2021–2026)
to answer key business questions about top-selling products, customer demographics, revenue trends, and return drivers. Data sourced from Kaggle
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/hyerdrac/retail-data?resource=download

Data:
A relational dataset with 5 tables: customers, orders, order_details, products, categories.

Business Questions:
1. What was the best-selling product in 2025 (by units sold and by revenue)?
2. Who is the typical customer buying the top revenue-generating product (city, gender, segment)?
3. How has revenue trended over time?
4. What is the return rate, and what are the main causes?

Tech:
SQL (SQLite) Python pandas matplotlib
